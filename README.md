# PathOS

**EXPLORE WHAT'S NEXT.**

PathOS is a career intelligence platform being built to help people understand their current career state, discover realistic career paths, identify the gaps between where they are and where they want to go, and build a clear path forward.

The long-term goal is to evolve PathOS into an intelligent career companion that can understand a person through conversation, reason about their career options, explain why a path fits them, identify what they are missing, and continuously guide them through learning, building, and career progression.

---

## The Final Goal

PathOS is ultimately intended to become a personal career operating system.

A user should be able to come to PathOS without already knowing exactly what career they want.

PathOS should be able to understand:

```text
Who you are
      ↓
Where you are
      ↓
What you know
      ↓
What you have built
      ↓
What you are good at
      ↓
What you want
      ↓
What constraints you have
      ↓
Where you could realistically go
