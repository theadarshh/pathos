# PathOS

## EXPLORE WHAT'S NEXT.

PathOS is a career intelligence platform designed to help people understand their current career position, explore realistic career directions, identify the skills they are missing, and turn a career goal into a structured path forward.

A career is rarely a straight line.

People often know where they are, or where they want to go, but struggle to understand the connection between the two.

PathOS is being built to bridge that gap.

Instead of providing a generic list of careers or a simple AI-generated recommendation, PathOS aims to understand a person's:

- Current role
- Professional experience
- Technical and transferable skills
- Existing strengths
- Career interests
- Career goals
- Skill gaps
- Learning progress
- Project experience
- Career constraints

and progressively transform that information into an actionable career path.

---

# The Problem

Career decisions are often fragmented across many different tools and sources.

A person may use:

- Career websites to discover roles
- Job boards to understand requirements
- Courses to learn skills
- YouTube or documentation to study
- Resume tools to present experience
- Project tutorials to build portfolios
- AI assistants to ask questions
- Spreadsheets to track progress

But these systems rarely understand the complete journey.

A user may know:

> "I want to become a DevOps Engineer."

But still not know:

- How close am I to that role?
- Which of my existing skills transfer?
- Which skills am I missing?
- Which skill should I learn first?
- Which skills can wait?
- What should I build to prove those skills?
- How long might the transition take?
- Am I actually ready to apply?
- Should I consider another path instead?
- What should I do this week?

PathOS is being built around this problem.
